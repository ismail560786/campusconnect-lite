## Quick Formatting Tips

1. Use a clean built-in theme (View → Theme → "Simple Light" or "Shift")
2. Keep bullet text 24pt or larger for readability on screen-share
3. Don't paste full paragraphs — say the extra detail out loud using the script; slides stay short
4. Slide 7 (Live Demo) should stay visually simple since you switch away from it quickly
5. Once done: **Share (top-right) → "Anyone with the link" → Viewer → Copy link** — this is your Presentation Slides Link for submission
